## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| <img src="docs/images/hands.jpg" alt="A Lifelike hand with Henry's wristwatch in the theatre" width="100%"> | <img src="docs/images/doors.jpg" alt="Henry's hand pushing a door open" width="100%"> |
| **Your hands, your body.** Look down and see Henry: cartoon gloves, braces and big shoes in the game's own style (or a Lifelike style), with a real wristwatch. Your arms are his arms. | **Doors by hand.** Push, pull or fling a door open; locked ones rattle and stop you. |
| <img src="docs/images/valve.jpg" alt="A gloved hand gripping a valve wheel" width="100%"> | <img src="docs/images/touch.jpg" alt="A finger booping a Bendy cut-out on the nose" width="100%"> |
| **Turn it yourself.** Valves and wheels turn in your hand as you wind them round; levers swing as you pull them; switches flip under your fingers. | **Touch everything.** Press buttons, DOOR RELEASE and tape recorders with a fingertip, lift lids, open drawers and chests, play the organ and the piano, boop Bendy on the nose. |
| <img src="docs/images/soup.jpg" alt="A can of Bacon Soup in Henry's gloved hand" width="100%"> | <img src="docs/images/darts.jpg" alt="A dart in Henry's hand, the dartboard across the room" width="100%"> |
| **Bacon Soup by hand.** Pick up a can, pull the lid off with your other hand, and tip it to your mouth. | **Throw for real.** Darts, soup cans and balls fly the way your hand moved, as hard as you threw them; at the fairground, point at a bottle and the ball lands where you point. |
| <img src="docs/images/fight.jpg" alt="The axe bursting a Searcher into ink" width="100%"> | <img src="docs/images/ink.jpg" alt="Ink stains on Henry's sleeve and glove" width="100%"> |
| **Fight like you mean it.** Swing the axe with one hand or both: a hit counts anywhere on an enemy, and planks break where you strike. Guns aim where your hand points, with a two-handed grip. | **Real-time ink.** Every drop lands on your hands, arms and clothes, runs and drips. Wade in and the ink ripples and splashes round you, and inky shoes leave footprints. |
| <img src="docs/images/scenes.jpg" alt="A scripted scene playing around you in VR" width="100%"> | <img src="docs/images/theatre.jpg" alt="The 1930s theatre the menus play in, Henry's glove raised" width="100%"> |
| **Cutscenes in VR.** Scripted scenes play around you while you keep Henry's body. Your head does the looking, and the world never tilts, spins or shakes. | **A picture palace.** The menus play on the big screen of a 1930s theatre built in the game's style, and VR SETTINGS sits inside the game's own Options. Click the move stick to walk it: the stage, the lobby, the stairs and the balcony, with a few things to find, and a mirror in the lobby where you change who you are. |

Also included:
- Every chapter playable from start to finish, and the Archives.
- Thirteen body styles: Henry (Lifelike or Toon), Bendy, Boris, Alice Angel, the Ink Demon, Sammy Lawrence, the Projectionist, Brute Boris, a Searcher, Joey Drew, Wally Franks and Beast Bendy. Eight of them are the game's own character models, read from your installation as it starts (nothing from the game ships with InkVR), worn as one connected body that moves with yours. Ink gets onto them, and the mirror in the theatre's lobby shows you who you are (point at its arrows and pull the trigger, or press them, to change).
- Six watches for your wrist, all keeping your real time (or none).
- **Optional:** a satchel over your left shoulder (off unless you turn it on: SATCHEL in VR SETTINGS or the launcher). With it on, what you pick up stays in your hand until you let go of it behind your left shoulder, which puts it in; reach over your shoulder and grip to bring the satchel round and take things out, hold one to what it's for and let go to use it, or throw it (it comes back).
- Natural grips: the axe, the tools and what you carry sit in your hand by their handles, and your fingers close round them.
- Solid weapons: the weapon in your hand stops at walls and floors (it tips back in your fist like a real one), your fingers close over its handle, and swings knock loose things about.
- Your shadow: Henry's body, arms and hands cast a real-time shadow under the game's lights.
- Tape notes come up close in front of you to read.
- Use things from where you stand: point at them with either hand, or just look at them, and grip or press X. Further off, point and grip and an item flies to your hand on an ink thread, and things that stay put (valves, levers, buttons, tape recorders) are used from there.
- Motion controls are never required: keep gripping a valve or a lever for a moment and it's done.
- A weapon wheel (click the right stick) and an inventory card in your hand (hold Y), or the game's own buttons.
- Snap or smooth turning; seated or standing play.
- World size: AUTO makes Henry's eyes your eyes, or set it yourself.
- Body options: full body, arms and hands, hands only, or none; any of the thirteen styles.
- Each motion control can be switched back to a button in VR SETTINGS.
- Comfort: calm the theatre's projector flicker and film scratches.
- Automatic render resolution to hold the headset's frame rate.
- An animated launcher that installs, checks and uninstalls everything for you.

## What you need

- **Bendy and the Ink Machine on Steam** (PC).
- **A PC VR setup:** Virtual Desktop, Quest Link / Air Link, or SteamVR. InkVR uses OpenXR, with bindings for
  Meta Quest (Touch) controllers and Valve Index controllers. It was made and tested on a Meta Quest 3 streaming
  through Virtual Desktop; other headsets may work but are untested.

## Install

1. [Download the latest release](../../releases/latest) and unzip it into a normal folder (Desktop, Documents,
   `C:\Games\InkVR`). Keep `InkVR Launcher.exe` and the `InkVR-Payload` folder together. Very deep folders
   (paths over 260 characters) stop Windows from starting the launcher.
2. Start your VR runtime first (on Quest 3: the Virtual Desktop Streamer, then connect from the headset).
3. Run **`InkVR Launcher.exe`**. It finds the game through Steam and installs InkVR the first time (it downloads
   Unity's OpenXR plugin from Unity).
4. Choose **PLAY IN VR**.

**VR CHECK** in the launcher shows what's installed and anything that needs fixing.
`"InkVR Launcher.exe" --play vr` starts the game straight away, for a desktop shortcut.

## Controls

| Left controller | Right controller |
|---|---|
| Stick: move (click it to run; in the menu theatre, click it to walk around) | Stick: turn (click it for the weapon wheel) |
| Trigger: run | Trigger: use your weapon, or swing your arm to hit |
| X: interact | A: jump |
| Y: inventory (hold it) | B: back, in menus |
| Grip: grab / use | Grip: grab / use |
| Menu: pause | |

Menus (the main menu, pause, Options and VR SETTINGS): point the laser from your hand and pull the trigger, or use
the stick and A. In the menu theatre, click the move stick to walk around it and again to go back to the menu.

Left-handed? Switch DOMINANT HAND in VR SETTINGS and the two sides swap. The launcher's **CONTROLS** page shows
the layout on a picture of the controllers, as you have it set up.

In the game, VR SETTINGS is in the game's own **Options** menu.

## If something looks wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The world looks too big or too small | VR SETTINGS: WORLD SIZE. AUTO reads your height while you stand up straight, and uses it from the next level or when you resume. |
| Henry's body gets in the way | VR SETTINGS, HENRY: switch BODY to arms and hands, hands only, or none. |
| It stutters | VR SETTINGS, GRAPHICS: turn on AUTO RESOLUTION, or lower RENDER SCALE. |
| The launcher won't start | Move the folder somewhere shorter (see Install, step 1). |
| A motion control fights you | VR SETTINGS, CONTROLS: switch it back to the button. |

## Reporting a bug

Bug reports help a lot. [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and attach:

- your log: `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` in the game's folder (in Steam, right-click the game, **Manage**,
  **Browse local files**). Close the game first.
- where in the game it happened, plus a short clip or a description;
- your headset and how you connect it (Virtual Desktop, Quest Link or SteamVR).

## Uninstall

Run `InkVR Launcher.exe`, open **OPTIONS** and choose **UNINSTALL INKVR**. It removes InkVR, the OpenXR plugin and
BepInEx's loader from the game folder. The game itself is not touched.

## Building from source

- The .NET SDK (6 or later), and Git Bash to run the scripts in `tools/`.
- The game, with InkVR's BepInEx installed and the game started once (BepInEx then generates the interop
  assemblies the plugin builds against).
- Set `INKVR_GAME_DIR` to the game's folder, then run `tools/build.sh` (the plugin) or `tools/package.sh`
  (the launcher, the payload and the release zip).

How it works inside, the roadmap and every build's test notes are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and
[docs/TESTING.md](docs/TESTING.md). `tools/vr-start.sh` runs the game against Unity's OpenXR mock runtime, so most
things can be tested without a headset.
