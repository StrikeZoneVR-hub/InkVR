# Changelog

## InkVR 1.1: The Characters Update

### New

- **Be the characters themselves.** Eight styles are now the game's own character models, head to toe: the Ink
  Demon, Sammy Lawrence, the Projectionist, a Searcher, Boris, Alice Angel (as Chapter 3 shows her, halo and all),
  Brute Boris and Bendy. Each is one connected body moved by yours: it stands, crouches and steps where you do, its
  arms reach your hands, its own fingers close when you grip, and your watch sits on its wrist. In first person you
  see its own arms, gloves and body; the mirror in the theatre's lobby and your shadow show all of it.
- **Read from your own game.** The models come from your installation as the game starts (some from the game's own
  files, the rest from the statues in its Archives). Nothing from the game ships with InkVR.
- **Bendy** is the studio's concept model of him (the Archives' statue), given a skeleton, with InkVR's drawn head:
  the statue's own head is a cutaway showing the works inside.
- **Ink gets onto them too:** wade through ink as Sammy and his trouser legs come out black.
- **The Projectionist's lens** throws light where you look, flickering like a projector.

### Quality of life

- **Point at the mirror's arrows.** In the theatre lobby, point either hand at the brass panel beside the mirror: a
  laser shows where you're pointing, and the arrow under it sinks a little. Pull that hand's trigger to change your
  style or your watch. Pressing the arrows with a finger still works.
- **Henry's shoulders.** The drawn styles (Henry, Toon Henry, Wally Franks, Joey Drew) have shoulders that move
  with the arms, so a raised arm grows out of the body instead of turning in a doll's socket.

### Bug fixes

- **Tape notes weren't up close, so they were hard to read.** In 1.0 the close-up page never came up: InkVR read
  where the game's note sits on the screen wrongly (as one point in the middle), took it for nothing, and left the
  note small in the far HUD, pixelated and blurry. Now, when a tape plays, its note comes up as a page half a metre
  in front of you, a little larger than 1.0 meant it to be.
- **Sharper text everywhere.** The game's menus, HUD and notes are drawn at 4K (they were 1440p), so a tape's note
  has about 1100 x 1450 pixels of its own, more than the page needs. The laser clicks just as before.

### Install or update

Download **InkVR-v1.1.0.zip** below, unzip it, and run `InkVR Launcher.exe`. It updates an existing install by
itself. Your settings are kept.

## InkVR 1.0

The biggest update yet, and the first full release. Everything since 0.9.1 is in it.

### New

- **Walk the theatre.** Click the move stick in the menu theatre to walk about, and click again for the menu: the
  hall, the stage, the lobby, the twin staircases and the balcony, with a few surprises to find. Your body walks with
  you, with quiet footsteps on the boards and the carpet. WALK THE THEATRE in VR SETTINGS or the launcher.
- **A mirror in the lobby**, by the piano. See yourself head to toe, and press the arrows on the brass panel beside
  it to change your style and your watch.
- **Thirteen body styles.** Be Henry (lifelike or toon), or step into the shoes of Bendy, Boris, Alice Angel, the Ink
  Demon, Sammy Lawrence, the Projectionist, Brute Boris, a Searcher, Joey Drew, Wally Franks or Beast Bendy. Each has
  its own hands, arms, body and head (you see the head in the mirror and in your shadow), and ink gets onto all of
  them. Some have extras: Bendy's and Boris's tails swing as you move, Alice's halo glows, the Projectionist's lens
  lights the way, and the ink creatures drip and leave inky footprints. Choose at the lobby mirror, in VR SETTINGS or
  in the launcher.
- **Six watches** for your wrist, all keeping your real time: Henry's tank watch, Bendy's character watch, the
  studio's gold watch, an ink-machine gauge whose vial fills as the chapters get inkier, Alice's angel watch with a
  little halo, and the Ink Demon's. Or none.
- **Your shadow.** Henry's body, arms and hands cast a real-time shadow that moves with you, wherever the game has a
  light that casts shadows. Fingers included. YOUR SHADOW in VR SETTINGS turns it off.
- **Throw what you carry.** Let go of anything you're holding mid-swing and it flies: gears, batteries, keys,
  Bacon Soup off the shelf. It lands where it lands.
- **Use things out of reach.** The gravity pull now works on fixed things too. Point at a valve, a lever, a
  switch, a button or a tape recorder across the room and grip: it's used from where you stand. Up close, your
  hands still do the work.
- **Solid weapons.** Your weapon is part of the world now. It stops at walls and floors instead of sinking into
  them: it tips back in your fist the way a real one would, and your controller buzzes against the wall. Swings
  knock loose things about (pool balls, broken bits), but nothing the story needs. WEAPONS in VR SETTINGS turns it off.
- **A satchel (optional, off by default).** Turn on SATCHEL in VR SETTINGS or the launcher, and it hangs over your
  left shoulder. Let go of an item behind your shoulder to put it in, then reach over your shoulder and grip to bring
  the satchel round and take things out. Hold one to what it's for and let go to use it. Throw one and it comes back.

### Quality of life

- **Point at it, or look at it, to use it.** Point at something with either hand, or just look at it, and the
  game's own highlight and prompt appear; grip or X uses it. No more walking right up to things before the prompt
  shows. With your hand on something, the hands-on way still works.
- **Chapter 4's fairground games by hand.** The ball toss: point at a bottle and pull the trigger, and the ball
  leaves your hand on the arc that lands where you point (a gold arc shows it); swing as you let go if you like. The
  shooting gallery: the pellet gun is in your hand with a gold sight line, and the trigger fires where it points.
- **Motion controls are never required.** Keep gripping a valve or a lever for a moment and it's done, however
  your hand moved. Pulling something to your hand starts as soon as you grip (no flick needed).
- **Laser pointer on by default** for the main menu, the pause menu, Options and VR SETTINGS. The stick and A still
  work, and the laser is steadier: it holds still as you squeeze the trigger. Point and click works in every menu,
  even after you've used the stick.
- **Gravity pull reaches story items.** Hearts, keys, batteries, books, gears, reels and the rest now fly to your
  hand, not just the few it knew before.
- **Seated play:** the gravity pull reaches 8.5 m and forgives a looser aim, since you can't lean over to things.
- **Natural grips.** The axe sits in your fist by its handle, and your other hand closes on the handle when you hold
  it with both, with your arm following. Tools, items and soup cans settle into your hand by their longest side.
  GRIPS in the launcher puts back the game's way of holding them.
- **A quiet axe.** It whooshes once per real swing, louder the harder you swing. Moving it about, or bumping a wall
  with it, just buzzes the controller.
- **Tape notes up close.** When a tape plays, its note (who's speaking, what they say) comes up in front of you as a
  sharp page to read. The menus and HUD are drawn sharper too. TAPE NOTES in the launcher puts it back in the HUD.
- **New options:** STYLE (thirteen styles), WATCH, WALK THE THEATRE, WEAPONS, SATCHEL, YOUR SHADOW in VR SETTINGS;
  STYLE, WATCH, WALK THE THEATRE, TAPE NOTES, SATCHEL and GRIPS in the launcher. The launcher's controls page explains
  the new controls.
- **A faster start.** Henry's textures paint several times faster, so the game gets to the menu sooner.

### Bug fixes

- **Chapter 4 got stuck at the vent: the flashlight did nothing.** A door pushed open by hand didn't tell the game
  it had been opened the way the story listens for, so the story waiting on the stairwell door never moved on and the
  flashlight was never switched on. Doors opened by hand now tell the game just as pressing interact does, at every
  door in every chapter. (A grip at the vent could also go to the trunk across the room: see *Point at it, or look
  at it*.)
- **Chapter 4's axe sat in your hand the wrong way.** The game has copies of the axe turned differently inside
  (Chapter 4's is a quarter turn round from Chapter 2's), and one copy could even be held by its head. Every axe now
  sits the same: by its handle, the blade's flat across your palm, whichever copy it is.
- **Chapter 4's throwing cans sat sideways and upside down,** gripped like a stick and drawn far too big. They're
  held upright now, a soup can's size, your fingers round them. (These are the empty cans you throw; they can't be
  eaten in the game either. The full cans you eat are held as before.)
- **Chapter 4's fairground games couldn't be won in VR, so the story couldn't go on.** Both aim with the game's
  camera (your head, in VR): the ball always flew the way you were looking, whatever your hand did, and the gallery
  shot where you looked, with no gun in your hand. Both now aim with your hand (see *Chapter 4's fairground games by
  hand*).
- **Prompts only showed with your hand on things**, so reaching Chapter 4's bridge lever meant walking up to it in
  real life. Fixed (see *Point at it, or look at it*).
- **The weapon wheel sometimes wouldn't open** in Chapter 4 (an error in the log each time). Fixed.
- **The laser clicked the wrong thing, or nothing.** Once you'd moved the stick in a menu, the game switched to
  controller mode: pointing stopped moving the highlight, and a click went to whatever the stick picked last (a click
  on OPTIONS could even quit the game). Now, while the laser is on the screen, the game treats it as a mouse, so a
  click goes to what you point at, in every menu. Three smaller causes are fixed too: letting go of the trigger jolts
  your hand, which could slide the laser off a small button just as you clicked; a half-pulled trigger could flicker
  on and off; and after quitting to the menu, clicks could go nowhere.
- **Your hand looked see-through around a weapon.** The game draws the weapon over everything, so in VR it was
  painted over Henry's fingers and through walls. Now your fingers close over the handle.
- **The axe made a loud sound at every small movement.** Fixed (see *A quiet axe*).
- **Many story items couldn't be pulled to you**, so you had to bend down, stretch or walk over to them. Fixed.
- **Thousands of errors after quitting to the menu.** When you went back into a chapter, every ink splash and
  footprint failed in the background, which cost performance. Fixed.
- **Your shadow had no hands** in the Lifelike style. Fixed.
- **Tape notes were blurry**, small in the far HUD. Fixed (see *Tape notes up close*).
- **The theatre and Henry tripped for a frame when the game reset** after quitting to the menu. Fixed.
- **The launcher's controls page ran off the bottom.** Fixed.
